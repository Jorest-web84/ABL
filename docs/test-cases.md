# ABL teacher test set

Run these requests after changing the generation prompt or activity schemas. Record whether the generated activity is valid, readable, editable, saveable, publishable, and playable on a phone.

1. Multiple choice: Grade 7 photosynthesis, 5 questions, mostly easy.
2. Multiple choice: Grade 9 mathematics, linear equations, 8 questions with short explanations.
3. True or false: Grade 6 geography, map skills, 6 statements.
4. True or false: Grade 8 history, causes of World War I, 5 statements.
5. Flashcards: Grade 7 biology, cell organelles, 10 cards.
6. Flashcards: Grade 10 English, literary devices, 8 cards.
7. Matching: Grade 8 mathematics, algebra terms and definitions, 6 pairs.
8. Matching: Grade 7 natural science, food chains, 5 pairs.
9. Put in order: Grade 6 history, steps in the water cycle, 6 items.
10. Put in order: Grade 9 technology, scientific investigation process, 7 items.

For each result, check:

- The content matches the selected type.
- There are no duplicate options or terms.
- The activity can be directly edited and saved.
- Publishing creates a working `/a/[id]` link.
- A student can complete it without logging in.
- The score and review screen are understandable.
